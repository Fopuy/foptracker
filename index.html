12-week program

Program details transcribed from your uploaded PDF. Choose a week and workout to inspect its exercise prescriptions.

Warm-up protocol

General warm-up before every workout (5–10 minutes): light cardio, 10 reps per side of arm swings, arm circles, front-to-back leg swings, side-to-side leg swings, and optional cable external rotations (15 reps per side).

Exercise-specific warm-up

1 warm-up set: about 60% of planned working weight for 6–10 reps.

2 warm-up sets: about 50% for 6–10 reps, then 70% for 4–6 reps.

3 warm-up sets: about 45% for 6–10, 65% for 4–6, then 85% for 3–4 reps.

4 warm-up sets: about 45% for 6–10, 60% for 4–6, 75% for 3–5, then 85% for 2–4 reps.

Program notes from the PDF

Week 1 is an introductory/deload week: most sets are around RPE 7–9, leaving 1–3 reps in reserve. From week 2 onward, the program notes say the last set of each exercise is taken to failure, except intro/deload weeks. Follow the exercise-specific technique and RPE guidance shown in each workout.
